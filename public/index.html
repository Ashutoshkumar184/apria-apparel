05 / USEFUL TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

If you don’t see what you need, send APRIA a note below.

How does a bulk quote work?＋

Send the garment, estimated quantity, size and colour mix, decoration, target date and delivery location. APRIA will confirm minimum quantity, whether a sample is available, price by quantity and the estimated production and delivery schedule. This enquiry does not place an order; you can review the quote before deciding.

How are catalogue prices decided?＋

The listed prices are indicative retail prices. Final bulk and custom pricing depends on garment choice, quantity, decoration and packaging, and is confirmed after APRIA reviews your enquiry.

Can APRIA make clothing for my label?＋

Yes. Use the enquiry form to share your product specifications, expected quantity, branding and target date. APRIA will confirm whether the request is a fit.

Can I request printing or embroidery?＋

Yes. Include the garment, placement, technique, quantity and any artwork details you have. APRIA will respond with available options.

How do I choose a size?＋

Open a garment to see its sizes and live availability. Exact garment measurements are not published yet. If you want sizing help, message APRIA with the garment name and the fit you prefer before ordering.

What should I know about delivery and returns?＋

The delivery fee is shown in your bag total. Delivery estimates and return or exchange eligibility depend on the garment and destination, so confirm the terms with APRIA before requesting payment. For custom, printed, embroidered or bulk work, confirm changes and cancellation terms in the quote before approving production. Ask APRIA about delivery or returns ↗

What happens after I submit checkout?＋

If secure UPI is available, Razorpay collects payment and APRIA verifies it. If UPI is unavailable, the button sends an unpaid order request only—no payment is taken. APRIA follows up to confirm stock, final total and delivery timing.

Can I change the size or colour in my bag?＋

Open the bag to review each item. To change its size or colour, remove it and choose the garment options again from the collection.

What if I’m only at the idea stage?＋

That’s a fine place to start. Share what you’re considering, even if quantity or details are still taking shape.
